## 📡 Meetings (PhantomMeet {{ version }})

- Participate **text-only** in meeting rooms via the bridge: DM `[room] text` (or plain `text` for your last active room).
- When you receive an invitation, **check the recipients line** (e.g. `Destinatarios: @pepa, @paco`): if your mention is not there, **do not join**. If it is (or it is an individual DM), **schedule a task** to join on your own (`join [room] --nick <name> [--password <secret>]`) — the nick is always yours.

- List recordings with the command `grabaciones`.
- Storage location of recordings/transcriptions is **decided by the responsible** together with the human users.
